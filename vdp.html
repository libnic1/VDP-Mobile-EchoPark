What's wired

Above the fold — swipe or drag the hero left/right to move through the photos; the dots between See Inside and the photo count track position. Double-tap the hero, or use the maximize control, to open the scaled full-screen state. The photo-count chip opens the Gallery. The Key Features / Included Upgrades toggle swaps the hero hotspots, and each hotspot opens its detail sheet with a working pager. See Inside opens the interior view; the location opens the nearby-lot sheet; Car Comparison opens the comparison and car-picker flow; Save toggles the count.

Buying options — three treatments from the wireframes: Progressive intent cards, Toggle-guided and Continuous scroll.

Below the fold — Options 01 and 02, with a sticky jump nav that tracks scroll position.
